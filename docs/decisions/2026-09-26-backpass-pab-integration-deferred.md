# Backpass–PAB integration: deferred

Date: 2026-09-26
Status: Deferred by repository owner; bridge preserved, not active on `main`.

**Decision sources:** [Owner discussion in g-chat 2](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-p-6aac315d34b88191a8d7cc529a7f94f8-g-chat-2/c/6aad2bcd-f850-83ea-b8f5-0a0b209f9639); [PAB ADR-0003 (authority question, rationale, and conditions for resumption)](https://github.com/AcidicSoil/personal-ai-bookmarks/blob/main/docs/adrs/0003-backpass-pab-authority-and-deferral.md); [preserved Backpass bridge](https://github.com/AcidicSoil/backpass/tree/park/backpass-pab-bridge-2026-09-26); [preserved PAB bridge](https://github.com/AcidicSoil/personal-ai-bookmarks/tree/park/backpass-pab-bridge-2026-09-26).

The original request, explicit subsequent user decisions, approved requirements, and acceptance criteria need provenance when evaluating task outcomes. Generated `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md` and skills define execution policy; they are not, by themselves, the complete definition of success. Sessions and artifacts are evidence, and PAB's saved resources are candidate methods.

The direct bridge exported Backpass's already proposed memory edits to PAB without an agreed intermediary to reconcile task intent, specs/plans, instruction policy, observed failures, and rejected/superseded decisions. Its contribution beyond the existing proposal was unverified, and its claim that rejected edits were filtered did not account for Backpass's separate human-rejection state. The bridge is therefore parked pending a reviewed design; no intermediary architecture or automatic delivery policy has been approved.

Do not restore the direct bridge merely by merging the parking branch. See PAB ADR-0003 for the complete decision boundary and resumption conditions. Backpass's normal scan, analysis, proposal, and reviewed apply workflow is unaffected.
